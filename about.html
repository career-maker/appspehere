What We Do

A platform founded in Kerala, built for India.

App Sphere B2B India Private Limited operates Source Pro B2B2C, founded and headquartered in Kerala. The platform enables kirana stores, local retailers, distributors, and manufacturers to participate in digital commerce while preserving existing business relationships.

The story behind Source Pro B2B2C

Neighbourhood stores have historically provided credit, employment, and relationships, but now face pressure from digital ordering expectations and quick-commerce platforms. Source Pro B2B2C aims to strengthen — not replace — traditional retail with the digital infrastructure it needs.

Our Mission, Vision & Goal

Mission

To empower every traditional Kirana store, distributor, and manufacturer in India with zero-investment digital tools that enable them to compete, grow, and thrive in the modern economy — without losing their identity, their community relationships, or their livelihoods.

Vision

A Digital India where every neighbourhood shop is a digital hypermarket. Where technology strengthens communities instead of replacing them. Where inclusive growth is not just an aspiration — it is the standard.

Goal

To build a structured and inclusive digital retail ecosystem that helps millions of retailers, distributors, and small entrepreneurs increase sales, formalise their business, create employment, and contribute more meaningfully to the nation’s economy — with a long-term goal of enabling up to 10 million digitally empowered income earners and future taxpayers across India.

From the Founder's Desk

Founder's Message

India is witnessing one of the biggest shifts in the history of retail. Small retailers — the very people who kept our neighborhoods alive for generations — are slowly being pushed toward extinction. The grocery stores we grew up buying from, the stationery shops that supported our education, and the family-run businesses built through decades of sacrifice and trust now stand helpless before a new reality they were never prepared for.

Smartphones, cheap internet, digital payments, e-commerce, quick commerce, and now Artificial Intelligence have permanently changed how India shops. Customers today expect instant delivery, endless product choices, online ordering, digital payments, and convenience at the touch of a button. Meanwhile, the traditional retailer — operating with limited resources, limited technology, and wafer-thin margins — is expected to compete against billion-dollar corporations powered by automation, algorithms, warehousing networks, and massive funding.

This is no longer a level playing field.

But this is not merely a business problem. It is a national economic and social concern. India is not built only by large corporations; it is built by ordinary people trying to earn an honest living. For decades, retail became the backbone of survival for millions of Indian families. These businesses educated children, built homes, generated employment, and strengthened local economies. Yet today, many of these same people are asking themselves: “What is left for us in the future?” That question became the reason behind Source Pro B2B2C.

What emerged was not merely another software platform, but what we believe to be a world’s first-of-its-kind digital retail empowerment ecosystem built to modernize and protect traditional commerce through technology.

We believe India does not need to choose between technology and traditional business. We believe digital progress must include everyone. Source Pro B2B is our attempt to create that bridge — a bridge where retailers become digital hypermarkets, distributors operate smarter through seamless digital commerce, manufacturers gain deeper market visibility, and customers continue supporting local businesses while enjoying the convenience of online shopping, competitive pricing, and trusted neighborhood relationships.

There is no going back to the old retail model. Consumer behaviour has already changed permanently. Retailers who remain purely offline will face increasing pressure with every passing year. The only path forward is digital transformation — but transformation should not mean displacement; it should mean inclusion. At Source Pro B2B, we envision a future where even the smallest neighborhood shop can access the same digital power once available only to large corporations. A future where technology strengthens communities instead of replacing them.

What makes this situation even more alarming is that India today stands at the crossroads of two massive transformations — the rise of AI-driven disruption across industries and the nation’s ambition to become the world’s third-largest economy. At a time when automation may gradually reduce traditional employment opportunities, India cannot afford the collapse of millions of small entrepreneurial livelihoods that form the backbone of its economy. We need more wealth creators, more taxpayers, stronger local economies, and higher per capita income — not economic concentration in the hands of a few dominant players.

This journey is much bigger than business. It is about protecting livelihoods, preserving dignity, empowering entrepreneurs, and building a digital India that leaves nobody behind.

We invite retailers, distributors, manufacturers, customers, investors, and every believer in inclusive growth to join us in this mission.

Because the future of retail must not only be digital — it must also remain human.

Jaideep OommenFounder Director & CEO, Source Pro B2B2C
