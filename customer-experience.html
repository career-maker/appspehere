The Customer Experience

Here Is What Your Customer Experience Looks Like.

Source Pro B2B2C gives retailers the clarity and confidence to introduce it to their customers in a way that is natural and compelling.

From the moment customers hear that your shop is now digital to the moment they place their first order — and every order after that — the experience is designed to feel simple, familiar, and trustworthy.

With Source Pro B2B2C, customers do not have to choose between online convenience and neighbourhood trust. They can continue buying from the retailer they already know, while enjoying the comfort of digital ordering.

HOW YOUR CUSTOMERS FIND YOU

Your customers will not discover your digital shop through a push notification or a sponsored advertisement. They will hear about it from you — the person they already trust. A WhatsApp message to your top 100 regulars. A mention across the counter the next time they visit. A simple sentence:

"My shop too is online now — you can order from me anytime through your phone."

That is all it takes to get started. The years of trust you have built with your customers are, in this moment, more powerful than any marketing budget. No quick-commerce platform in the world can replicate that opening line — because none of them have the relationship you already have.

Once these customers start ordering and share the experience with neighbours and family, your network grows organically. In a tight-knit community, word travels fast — and it costs you nothing.

Here Is What Your Customer Experience Looks Like

  1. 1They Discover That Your Shop Is Now DigitalYou contact your existing customers directly — This can happen through your WhatsApp message, shop display, personal invitation, QR code, social media sharing, or word of mouth. They hear about your digital shop from you, not from an algorithm. That personal introduction is what makes every subsequent interaction different from a faceless online platform.
  2. 2They Browse Products from HomeOne free download from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Works on any standard Android or iOS smartphone. No technical knowledge needed — for your customer. Customers can view a wide range of products from the comfort of their home. Instead of calling repeatedly, visiting the shop physically, or depending only on what they see on the shelf, they can explore products digitally at their convenience. Your shop becomes accessible beyond normal shop hours, giving customers the freedom to browse and order when it suits them.
  3. 3They Order from a Retailer They Already TrustThe moment they log in, they see your storefront — your shop name, your product catalogue, and the prices you have set for them. Not a marketplace with thousands of competing sellers. They know where the shop is. They know whom to contact. They know who is accountable. This gives them greater comfort, especially for daily essentials and household requirements. Familiar and personal from the first screen.
  4. 4They Enjoy Online Convenience Without Leaving Local SupportA traditional shop may have limited shelf space, but a digital storefront can display a much wider catalogue. 25,000+ products with images, descriptions, and transparent pricing — available anytime, from anywhere. Your customers can place an order at 10 PM on a Sunday, during a festival, or before your shop opens in the morning. Every hour your physical shop is closed is now an hour your digital shop is still earning.
  5. 5They Receive Better Service and Local AccountabilityYou receive the order, source it from your area distributor, and deliver it locally — under your own invoice, with payments collected via UPI or cash. Your customer receives their order from someone they know and trust. That last-mile experience builds trust, strengthens local relationships, and creates a more personal buying experience that customers genuinely value. They are supporting local businesses, local employment, and community-based commerce.

WHY THEY CHOOSE YOU OVER THE GIANTS

Your customers did not discover you through a performance marketing campaign. They found you through years of living in the same neighbourhood, buying from you on ordinary days and difficult ones, and calling you directly when something went wrong. That accumulated trust is the single most powerful asset your business has — and the moment your prices and convenience are comparable, it makes the choice obvious.

If a customer receives the wrong item from a large online platform, they often have to navigate chatbots, support tickets, and waiting times. If they receive the wrong item from you, they simply call you and the issue gets resolved quickly. Your credit facility — the arrangement you have always had with your most trusted customers — continues exactly as it always has. These are not small advantages. They are the reasons your customers stayed with you before Source Pro B2B2C, and the reasons they will stay with you now that you are digital.

Conveniently Many of your customers are aware of what is happening to local shops. They want to support you — but they also want the convenience they have grown used to. Source Pro B2B2C gives them both at once. A guilt-free, easy, and genuinely convenient way to shop local every single time.

WHAT YOUR CUSTOMERS GET

  • 25,000+ Products at Their Fingertips A catalogue that rivals the biggest e-commerce platforms, through a shop they already know.
  • Credit Facility Still Available Trusted customers continue with the credit arrangements you have always honoured.
  • Competitive Pricing, 24/7 Ordering Matching or beating major platforms anytime — day, night, weekends, and holidays.
  • Personal Service and Direct Accountability Any issue resolved directly with you — immediately, without a helpdesk or chatbot.
