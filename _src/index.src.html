FAQ

Questions, answered.

More questions? Talk to the team

What is Source Pro B2B2C?

A mobile-application-based digital commerce ecosystem created to empower India's traditional retailers, distributors, manufacturers, and end customers. It enables B2B2C trade between distributors and retailers and also enables retailers to serve their own customers digitally through a B2C flow.

Why is Source Pro the need of the hour?
  • Self-employment is vital to sustaining families.
  • Traditional retailers face pressure from digital adoption, quick-commerce and rising costs.
  • Protecting self-employment is an economic and social responsibility.
  • Source Pro enables retailers to sell a wide range without heavy stock investment.
How is Source Pro different from ordinary e-commerce?

Most online platforms sell directly to end customers, often bypassing the traditional retailer. Source Pro B2B2C is different. It is built around the existing retail and distribution ecosystem.

Is retail still viable in the age of AI and automation?

Daily essentials — food, household items, personal care, groceries — are among the few categories that no level of automation or AI can eliminate.

What products can be listed?

Categories include stationery, groceries, cosmetics, fashion, electronics, baby care, pet care, home care, toys, beverages, and other daily-use products.
