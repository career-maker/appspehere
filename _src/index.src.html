For generations, India's 13 million+ Traditional stores have been the heartbeat of local communities — providing daily essentials, credit, employment, and trust to millions of families across every town, street, and neighbourhood.

This is no longer just a business challenge. It is a livelihood challenge affecting millions of families across India.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Source Pro B2B2C, its purpose, features and how it empowers India’s traditional retail ecosystem.

What is Source Pro B2B2C?

Source Pro B2B2C is a mobile-application-based digital commerce ecosystem created to empower India’s traditional retailers, distributors, manufacturers, and end customers. It enables B2B2C trade between distributors and retailers and also enables retailers to serve their own customers digitally through a B2C flow. In simple terms, Source Pro B2B2C helps a small local shop become a digital hypermarket without losing its identity, ownership, customer relationships, or margin potential.

Why is Source Pro the need of the hour?

India is a nation of nearly 1.5 billion people, where self-employment has always played a vital role in sustaining families, communities, and the local economy. For millions of people, running a shop is not just a business — it is a livelihood, a source of dignity, and often the most practical path to financial independence.

Today, traditional retailers are facing pressure from multiple directions. Rapid digital adoption, online marketplaces, quick-commerce platforms, changing customer behaviour, artificial intelligence-led disruption, rising operating costs, and global supply chain uncertainties are all reshaping the way business is done.

At the same time, it is not practical for any country to provide formal employment to every household. Therefore, protecting and strengthening self-employment opportunities such as retail businesses is not just an economic necessity, but a social responsibility.

Recent global events have shown how deeply connected local businesses are to international developments. Conflicts thousands of miles away, trade policy changes, fuel price shocks, shipping disruptions, and commodity shortages can affect transportation costs, product availability, and business continuity within weeks. We have already seen how disruptions in fuel, LPG, and essential supply chains can bring dependent businesses to a grinding halt.

The world is also moving towards stronger “nation-first” policies. As countries prioritise employment and opportunities for their own citizens, many Indians working abroad may also face uncertainty and may eventually return to India in search of livelihood opportunities. This makes it even more important for India to build strong, scalable, and inclusive self-employment models within the country.

Source Pro B2B2C is built for this changing reality. It does not aim to replace traditional retailers with technology. Instead, it empowers them with the digital infrastructure, product access, distributor support, and supply chain strength required to remain competitive in the modern economy.

Through a distributor-led, demand-driven supply model, Source Pro helps retailers sell a wide range of products without heavy stock investment, operate both offline and online, serve their existing customers digitally, and stay relevant in an increasingly unpredictable world.

Modernisation should not mean the complete annihilation of an entire sector that supports millions of families. It should mean inclusion, empowerment, and transformation.

Source Pro is the need of the hour because it offers a practical bridge between traditional retail and the digital future — protecting livelihoods, creating new employment opportunities, strengthening local trade, and helping India build a more resilient, inclusive, and self-reliant retail ecosystem.

Why was Source Pro B2B2C created?

Source Pro B2B2C was created because traditional retailers are facing an unprecedented challenge from e-commerce and quick-commerce platforms. Customers now expect wider product choice, online convenience, transparent pricing, quick delivery, and attractive discounts. Most small retailers cannot independently build and manage a full digital platform. Source Pro B2B2C bridges this gap by giving them ready access to technology, products, digital ordering, and distribution support.

How is Source Pro B2B2C different from ordinary e-commerce platforms?

Most online platforms sell directly to end customers, often bypassing the traditional retailer. Source Pro B2B2C is different. It is built around the existing retail and distribution ecosystem. The distributor continues to supply, the retailer continues to sell, and the customer continues to buy through a trusted local shop – but with the convenience of modern digital commerce.

Is Source Pro B2B2C a first-of-its-kind initiative?

Source Pro B2B2C is positioned as a world’s first-of-its-kind retailer empowerment initiative that combines B2B2C distribution, retailer-led B2C selling, digital catalog access, territory-based distribution, and customer ordering through local shops in one integrated mobile ecosystem. App Sphere B2B India Private Limited has also applied for patent protection for this business model and technology-enabled process.

Is retail still a viable business opportunity in the age of AI and automation?

Daily essentials — food, household items, personal care, groceries — are among the few categories that no level of automation or AI can eliminate. People need these products every single day, regardless of what is happening in the economy or the job market. In fact, as AI-driven disruption reshapes employment across industries and traditional job security weakens, self-employment through retail becomes one of the more resilient paths available. Source Pro B2B is built around exactly this reality — giving retailers and distributors a digitally powered, demand-driven business built on something that will never go away: the daily needs of every household in India.

What is the core vision of Source Pro B2B2C?

The vision is clear: digital growth must be inclusive, not exclusive. India should not have to choose between technology and traditional business. Source Pro B2B2C aims to create a bridge where retailers become digital hypermarkets, distributors operate smarter, manufacturers gain deeper market visibility, and customers enjoy modern convenience while continuing to support local businesses.

What is the tagline or philosophy behind Source Pro B2B2C?

The philosophy is: For The People, By The Distributor, Through The Retailer aka “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam” This mean The World Is One Family.

The customer benefits, the distributor remains relevant, and the retailer becomes digitally empowered instead of being replaced.

What types of products can be listed on Source Pro B2B2C?

The platform is designed to support a very wide range of categories including school stationery, office stationery, general stationery, groceries, cosmetics, fashion accessories, electronics, home appliances, baby care, pet care, home care, arts and crafts, food and beverages, toys, and many other daily-use and lifestyle products. The objective is to make tens of thousands of products digitally accessible through local retailers.

Is Source Pro B2B2C easy to use?

Yes. Source Pro B2B2C is designed for ordinary shopkeepers, distributors, and customers. A retailer does not need to be technically skilled or invest in building a separate website or app. With a smartphone and guided onboarding, they can start using the platform and begin serving customers digitally.

How does Source Pro B2B contribute to India's broader economy?

Every retailer that Source Pro B2B empowers becomes a more formal, more productive, and more visible part of India’s economy. As digital orders grow, retailers generate documented income, file income tax returns, employ local delivery staff, and contribute to GST collections — turning what were often informal micro-businesses into structured, tax-paying enterprises. At a time when India needs more wealth creators, more taxpayers, and stronger local economies to support its ambition of becoming the world’s third-largest economy, Source Pro B2B’s model is designed to create exactly that — at scale, across every constituency and district it operates in.

If the Green Revolution empowered the farmer and the White Revolution empowered the dairy producer, Source Pro B2B2C aims to empower the traditional retailer through the E-Revolution — strengthening one of the most important pillars of India’s self-employment economy.
