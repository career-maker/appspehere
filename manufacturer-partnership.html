Manufacturer questions, answered.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often from manufacturers considering Source Pro B2B2C.

Why should a manufacturer partner with Source Pro B2B2C?

Source Pro B2B2C gives manufacturers a digital route to reach thousands of retailers through an organized distributor network. It improves product visibility, reduces dependence on purely manual order collection, and helps brands participate in the digital transformation of traditional retail.

How does Source Pro B2B2C improve product visibility?

Once products are listed, they become visible to distributors, retailers, and, through retailers, end customers. Instead of relying only on sales executives visiting selected outlets, manufacturers can make their full product range discoverable digitally.

Can manufacturers list all SKUs?

Yes. Manufacturers can list their complete SKU range, subject to onboarding, commercial terms, product data, images, pricing, stock availability, and applicable statutory requirements. This enables retailers to discover products that may not be physically available in their shop at that moment.

How does the model support market intelligence?

Digital order data can help manufacturers understand product demand, regional sales patterns, customer preferences, fast-moving SKUs, and retailer response. This can support production planning, inventory planning, marketing strategy, and new product decisions.

Can Source Pro B2B2C reduce the need for large field sales teams?

The platform can act as a 24×7 digital sales channel for listed products. While brand-building and market activation may still be required, digital visibility and app-based ordering can reduce the limitations of manual visit-based selling.

How does Source Pro B2B2C help prevent stock dumping?

Because the model is built around demand visibility and digital ordering, brands can move toward better planning instead of pushing inventory blindly into the channel. This can reduce dead stock, expiry risk, and market dissatisfaction.

Can local and regional brands participate?

Yes. Source Pro B2B2C is designed to support both established and local brands. Local brands can gain wider visibility through the platform and reach retailers beyond their existing limited distribution footprint.

What advantage does a manufacturer get over traditional distribution?

Traditional distribution often leaves many outlets uncovered due to manpower limits and inconsistent sales visits. Source Pro B2B2C can digitally connect brands with a much wider retailer base while retaining the role of distributors and retailers in the supply chain.

How does the model help manufacturers support traditional retailers?

By partnering with Source Pro B2B2C, manufacturers can help their existing retailer base become digitally active rather than watching them lose customers to large online platforms. This creates a responsible, future-ready channel strategy.

Can manufacturers control pricing and product information?

The platform is designed to display structured product information and pricing across levels. Commercial pricing, margins, and price-list allocation can be managed as per the agreed framework and platform capability.

What is expected from manufacturers?

Manufacturers are expected to provide accurate product details, images, pricing, stock support, statutory compliance documents, and supply commitment. They are also encouraged to invest in brand awareness while Source Pro B2B2C supports digital availability through the retailer-distributor network.
